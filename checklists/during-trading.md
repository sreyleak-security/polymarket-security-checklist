# During-Trading Checklist (Polymarket)

Daily discipline while your account is funded and active.

## Signing discipline
- Read what you sign. Every time. Scam contracts rely on blind signing.
- - If a signature request does not match the action you initiated — reject it.
  - - One signature = one action. Batching approvals is how drains happen.
   
    - ## URL checks
    - - Navigate by bookmark, not by search results or links
      - - Character-by-character check after any redirect: polymarket.com
        - - Malicious clones differ by one character: rn vs m, 0 vs o
         
          - ## Session hygiene
          - - Sign out of sessions you do not use daily
            - - Session Key expired? Re-create it deliberately, do not auto-renew blindly
              - - Check active sessions weekly
               
                - ## Market sanity
                - - A market promising 'guaranteed returns' is not a market, it is bait
                  - - Before trading a new market, verify its contract address against official listings
                    - - If a Telegram/Discord admin DMs you first — it is a scam. Real admins do not DM first.
                     
                      - ## Money rules
                      - - Never keep more on the trading wallet than you plan to deploy this week
                        - - Withdraw profits on a schedule. Profits sitting in hot wallets feed scammers.
                          - - Keep a withdrawal test: small amount out, then in. Know the path before you need it in a hurry.
