# Post-Incident Checklist (Polymarket)

You clicked the link. You signed the thing. Here is what to do in the first 30 minutes - and what NOT to do.

## First 10 minutes: contain
- Stop trading. Do not 'test' whether the site still works - it may be the attacker's site.
- - If a transaction was signed that you did not initiate: your key is compromised. Treat it as gone.
  - - Move remaining funds from the affected wallet to a clean wallet (new key, never exposed).
   
    - ## Minutes 10-20: revoke
    - - Revoke token approvals you granted recently (attackers drain via approvals after the fact).
      - - Invalidate the Session Key if you used one.
        - - Check the address you interacted with on a block explorer - note everything for the report.
         
          - ## Minutes 20-30: report
          - - Report through official Polymarket support channels only (found via bookmark, not via links).
            - - Note: Polymarket refunded every victim of the June 25, 2026 frontend hack. Official response exists. Do not panic-hunt for 'helpers'.
             
              - ## What NOT to do
              - - Do NOT DM anyone who contacted you first. Real support never initiates.
                - - Do NOT give your seed phrase to anyone claiming to 'recover' funds. 100 percent of such offers are scams.
                  - - Do NOT pay an upfront fee to a 'recovery service'. That is the second scam taking the rest.
                    - - Do NOT post your transaction hash publicly with your handle attached.
                     
                      - ## The mindset
                      - Getting scammed does not make you stupid. Scammers are professionals. But the SECOND loss - paying a fake recovery service - is always avoidable.
                     
                      - ## After
                      - - Rebuild the wallet from a new seed. New paper. New place.
                        - - Re-read the pre-trading checklist before funding again.
