# Khmer Red Flags - ការព្រមានសម្រាប់សហគមន៍ខ្មែរ

Crypto scam warning patterns targeting Khmer speakers. Prepared by Srey Leak (son of Cambodian refugees, Long Beach CA) for community workshops.

## How scammers approach Khmer speakers
- They message first, in Khmer, on Facebook/Telegram/WhatsApp
- - They mention mutual community connections ('I know your cousin in Phnom Penh')
  - - They use official-looking logos with slightly wrong names
   
    - ## The universal scam sentence
    - If you hear ANY version of these - stop:
    - - 'បញ្ចូល seed phrase ដើម្បីទាញយកប្រាក់វិញ' (enter your seed phrase to recover funds)
      - - 'គ្រូសារ Polymarket ត្រូវការព័ត៌មានកន្ត្រងរបស់អ្នក' (Polymarket support needs your credentials)
        - - 'ចំណេញ 100%' (100 percent profit)
         
          - ## Facts to remember
          - - Polymarket itself refunded ALL victims of the June 25, 2026 hack
            - - Real support NEVER asks for seed phrases (ពាក្យសម្ងាត់ 12 ពាក្យ)
              - - Recovery services that ask for money first are the second scam
               
                - ## What to do instead
                - - Close the chat. Do not reply. Replying confirms your number is live.
                  - - Call your family member they mentioned - verify independently
                    - - Bring the message to a workshop or to someone who reads Khmer AND knows crypto
