# Lao Red Flags

Crypto scam warning patterns targeting Lao speakers. Prepared by Srey Leak (security researcher, Long Beach CA) for community workshops.

## Pattern
- First contact in Lao on Facebook/Telegram
- - Trust building through community references and temple events
  - - Fake screenshots of profits, slightly-off official names
   
    - ## The universal scam sentence
    - - Any request to enter your 12-word seed phrase to 'recover' or 'verify' funds
      - - 'Polymarket support needs your key' - real support never does
        - - Guaranteed profit promises
         
          - ## Facts
          - - Polymarket itself refunded all June 25, 2026 hack victims
            - - Seed phrase requests are ALWAYS scams, in any language
              - - Upfront-fee recovery services are the second scam
               
                - ## What to do
                - - Close and do not reply
                  - - Verify independently through people you know offline
                    - - Free workshops in Long Beach - bring the message, we review together
