# Vietnamese Red Flags - Canh bao cho cong dong Viet

Crypto scam warning patterns targeting Vietnamese speakers. Prepared by Srey Leak (security researcher, Long Beach CA) for community workshops.

## How scammers approach Vietnamese speakers
- First contact in Vietnamese, on Zalo/Facebook/Telegram
- - Reference to 'huynh de' networks or mutual acquaintances
  - - Screenshots of fake profits, real logos with wrong spellings
   
    - ## The universal scam sentence
    - - 'Nhap seed phrase de hoan tien' (enter seed phrase to get a refund)
      - - 'Bo phan ho tro Polymarket can xac thuc vi chi' (Polymarket support needs to verify your key)
        - - 'Loi nhuan dam bao 100%' (guaranteed 100 percent profit)
         
          - ## Facts
          - - Polymarket refunded ALL victims of the June 25, 2026 hack itself
            - - Real support NEVER asks for your 12-word phrase
              - - Anyone charging upfront for 'recovery' is the second scam
               
                - ## What to do
                - - Close the chat, do not reply
                  - - Verify the mutual acquaintance independently
                    - - Bring the message to a free workshop in Long Beach
